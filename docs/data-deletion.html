You can permanently delete your RockStyle Chess account and the personal data associated with it at any time. This page works whether or not you still have the app installed.

Option 1 — Delete it yourself, in the app

This is the fastest way, and takes effect immediately:

  1. Open RockStyle Chess and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account & Security.
  3. Scroll to Delete Account and confirm.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, or can't sign in, email us instead:

Send an email to royalriffstudio@gmail.com with the subject "Delete My Account", from the email address registered to your account (so we can verify it's really you). We process these requests within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Whichever option you use, the result is the same:

  • Your profile — display name, avatar, country, level, rating, and stats.
  • Your Chips and Gems balances, owned cosmetics, and progression (quests, achievements, daily-reward streak, spin history).
  • Your friendships, direct messages, and notifications.
  • Your purchase history and any reports you filed.

Matches you played remain in your opponents' match history for accuracy (so their record and rating stay correct), but your account is removed from that record rather than the match being deleted outright — the same behavior described in our Privacy Policy's Data Retention section.
