5. Chip Stakes, Festival Run and Other Chip Features

Several features let you spend Chips for a chance to win more Chips. They are part of the game only: Chips cannot be bought back, cashed out or exchanged for money or anything of real-world value, whatever the outcome, so nothing in the App is gambling with real money.

  • Staked venues. At venues with a buy-in, both players' buy-ins are held when the match is paired. The winner receives the prize shown before the match (the pot less a house fee of 10% of the pot, which is removed from the game); a draw returns both buy-ins. If a match ends by resignation, agreed draw or disconnection before each side has made three moves, it is void and both buy-ins are returned. If our server has to stop a live staked match (for example during maintenance), the match ends and both buy-ins are returned.
  • Safety Net. At a staked venue you may add an optional Safety Net for a Chip premium shown in the App. If you lose, it returns the share of your buy-in shown in the App; the premium is returned if the match is void and kept on a win or a draw.
  • Festival Run. You pay a Chip entry to play a ladder of sets against bots run by our server. Between sets you may cash out the purse banked so far or play on; a lost set ends the run and forfeits the purse not yet cashed out. Resigning, running out of time, or staying disconnected beyond the reconnection window loses the set. If a run is interrupted on our side, we pay out the purse you had banked or, if you had not banked anything yet, return your entry. Entry prices, daily entry limits and purses are shown in the App and may change for future runs.
  • Spins and Backstage Gear. The daily prize wheel is free once a day; extra spins cost Chips or an optional ad. Prizes are random, and the possible prizes are shown on the wheel. Backstage Gear items bought with Chips affect rewards only, never chess strength or rating.
  • The result recorded by our server is final for any payout. If a bug or outage causes an incorrect Chip balance, we may correct it.
