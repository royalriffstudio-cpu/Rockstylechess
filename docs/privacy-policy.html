Royal Riff Studio ("we," "us," or "our") operates RockStyle Chess (the "App"), a mobile chess game with real-time multiplayer, social, and progression features. This Privacy Policy explains exactly what information the App collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. It's written to match what the App's code actually does — not generic boilerplate — and is drafted to meet the requirements of the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") for players in the European Union / European Economic Area ("EEA"), as well as players everywhere else the App is available.

Short version: We collect what's needed to run your account, your matches, and your in-game purchases. The App shows ads served by Google AdMob (see Section 2i); there are no analytics SDKs, and the only diagnostics tool is an anonymous crash reporter (Sentry, see Section 2j). Push notifications are optional and only sent if you allow them (Section 2k). Where the law requires it, ads are only requested after you consent through Google's consent form, and a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase turns off the ads shown between games. We never sell your personal information.

1. Data Controller

For the purposes of the GDPR, the "data controller" responsible for your personal data is Royal Riff Studio, operating from Poland (a member state of the European Union), contactable at royalriffstudio@gmail.com for any privacy matter, including the requests in Section 7 below.

2. Information We Collect

a. Account information

  • Email address and password — used to create and secure your account. Your password is never stored in plain text; it is hashed before storage.
  • Email verification and password reset — when you sign up with an email address, or ask to reset your password, we email you a 6-digit code. The code is stored only until it is used or expires (10 minutes). To catch typing mistakes and throwaway addresses at sign-up, our server checks the address's format and looks up whether its domain can receive mail (a public DNS lookup of the domain only; nothing is sent to the address at that step).
  • Sign in with Google (optional) — if you choose "Continue with Google" or link Google in Account Security, Google tells us your Google account ID, email address, name and profile picture link, and that the email address is verified. We use the email to identify your account; your Google password is never shared with us.
  • Display name ("stage name") and avatar selection — shown to other players.
  • Country (optional) — a two-letter country code you may choose during setup, shown as a flag next to your name on leaderboards and in matches. You can skip this entirely.

b. Gameplay and profile data

  • Chess rating, level, XP, win/loss/draw record, and win streaks.
  • Match history, including the full move list (in standard PGN chess notation) and per-move timing, so you can review past games.
  • In-game currencies ("Chips" and "Gems"), owned cosmetic items (boards, piece sets, avatars), unlocked bots, quest/achievement progress, daily-reward streaks, and spin-wheel history.
  • Records of in-game Chip transactions: buy-ins at staked venues and how they were settled, Festival Run entries, sets and payouts, Backstage Gear and other Chip purchases — so balances stay correct and disputes can be checked.
  • Your friend code and friends list.

c. Social features

  • Friend requests and friendships between accounts.
  • Direct messages you send to friends within the App (message text and timestamps).
  • In-app notifications (e.g. friend requests, challenge invites, match results).

d. Player reports and blocking

If you report another player, we store who filed the report, who it's about, the reason you selected, any additional details you typed, and (if applicable) which match it relates to. If you block another player, we store that block so it can prevent messaging and hide each of you from the other.

e. Purchase information

If you buy Chips, Gems or the one-time "Remove Ads" upgrade, purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing — RockStyle Chess never sees or stores your payment card details. Our server verifies each purchase directly with Google's Play Developer API using a purchase token, and stores a record of: which pack or upgrade you bought, how much currency it granted (if any), the price, and Google's transaction identifier — purely to credit your account and prevent duplicate/fraudulent credits.

f. Device and session data

  • A session token that keeps you signed in, plus the IP address and browser/device "user agent" string associated with that session — used only for account security (e.g. detecting suspicious sign-ins), not by us for tracking or advertising.
  • Before you sign in, the App generates a random, anonymous guest identifier stored only on your device, used solely to let you queue for a match or reconnect to one before you have an account.
  • If you allow notifications: your device's push token, its platform (Android/iOS) and its time-zone offset, plus your notification settings (see Section 2k).

g. Information stored only on your device

Some data never leaves your device and is not visible to us:

  • Your authentication token, stored in your device's secure, encrypted credential storage (Android/iOS Keystore via Expo SecureStore).
  • Sound/music preferences, cached puzzle progress, how many games/puzzles you have finished since the last full-screen ad (to pace ads), whether you chose to play as a guest, a copy of your push token and whether you have seen the notification prompt, and the anonymous guest ID mentioned above, stored in the App's local on-device storage (AsyncStorage).

h. Real-time gameplay data

While you're in a match or using chat, your moves, presence status (online/in‑game/ offline), and messages are transmitted over a live connection to our own server to keep the board, clock, and conversations in sync between players. This traffic goes only to our server — never through a third party.

i. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows two kinds of ads served by Google AdMob: full-screen ads at natural pauses (after some finished games and between some puzzles) and optional "rewarded" ads you choose to watch in exchange for in-game chips or an extra spin/daily-bonus boost. Never during a live game. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK on your device collects and sends to Google: your device's advertising ID (which you can reset or opt out of in your device's Android settings), IP address, device and OS information, and ad interaction data. When you watch a rewarded ad, Google also calls our server to confirm the ad was watched; that callback carries your account's internal player ID, the ad's identifier and a transaction ID, and we store it only to prevent a reward being claimed twice.

If you are in the EEA or UK, the App first shows Google's consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) and requests ads only according to your choice; you can change that choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Ad Choices. Ads are requested as general-audience, not child-directed. Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects through the SDK — see how Google uses data from apps that use its services. A one-time "Remove Ads" purchase in the Shop turns off the full-screen ads; optional rewarded ads remain available.

j. Crash and error reports (Sentry)

To find and fix crashes, the App and our server send crash and error reports to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.). These reports are anonymous: we do not attach your account ID, email address, display name, chat messages, session token or the contents of requests. A report contains technical information only: the error message and stack trace, your device model and Android version, the App version, which screen you were on and recent navigation steps, whether the App was in the foreground, and performance information such as slow or frozen frames. A small share of sessions (about 10%) also includes screen-load and request timings. We do not record your screen, take screenshots, or capture what you type. Our server sends reports of its own errors (for example a failed match settlement) the same way, tagged with technical identifiers such as a match ID but not with player identities.

k. Push notifications (optional)

The App asks your permission before sending notifications, and you can turn them off at any time in your device settings or by category (social, invites, match results, daily reminder) in Settings. If you allow them, the App registers a push token for your device with the Expo push service, and our server stores that token with your account, your device's platform and time-zone offset (used only to send the daily-reward reminder in your evening). To deliver a notification, our server sends the token and the notification's text to Expo, which passes it to Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android) or Apple's push service (iOS). The text can include a friend's display name and the first part of a message they sent you. We skip a notification while the App is open on your screen. Signing in with another account on the same phone moves that phone's token to the new account; deleting your account deletes your tokens and notification settings.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

To be explicit, RockStyle Chess does not contain:

  • Any advertising SDK other than Google AdMob (Section 2i).
  • Any analytics or product-usage tracking SDK (e.g. no Firebase Analytics, no Mixpanel, no Amplitude, no Segment).
  • Any crash-reporting SDK other than Sentry (Section 2j), and no crash report ever carries your account details.
  • Any social-media login other than the optional Sign in with Google (Section 2a), and no tracking pixels.
  • Access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photo library, or precise location.

This reflects the current state of the App's code. If that ever changes — for example, adding a new third-party service — we will update this Policy first.

6. Data Retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account (Settings → Account & Security → Delete Account), we permanently delete your profile, currencies, cosmetics, friendships, messages, purchase records, push tokens and notification settings, Festival Run records, and progression data. Matches you played remain in your opponents' match history for accuracy, and so do the buy-in records of staked matches you played against them, but your account reference on those records is removed rather than deleted outright, so their history, balance records and rating stay intact. Email verification and password-reset codes are deleted once used or expired (10 minutes).

Crash and error reports (Section 2j) are anonymous and are kept by Sentry for 90 days, then deleted automatically.

7. Your Rights Under the GDPR

If you are in the EEA, UK, or otherwise protected by the GDPR, you have the following rights over your personal data. To exercise any of them, email royalriffstudio@gmail.com — we will respond within one month, as GDPR requires.

  • Right of access (Art. 15): ask us to confirm what personal data of yours we hold, and get a copy of it.
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16): correct inaccurate data. Most profile fields (display name, avatar, country, equipped cosmetics) can be corrected directly in the App.
  • Right to erasure / "right to be forgotten" (Art. 17): delete your account and associated personal data at any time from Settings → Account & Security → Delete Account, or by emailing us. This is immediate and permanent.
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18): ask us to pause processing your data in certain circumstances (e.g. while a dispute about its accuracy is resolved).
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20): receive the personal data you provided to us in a structured, commonly-used, machine-readable format, for data we process based on your consent or a contract.
  • Right to object (Art. 21): object to processing based on our legitimate interests (Section 4 above); we will stop unless we have compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)): where processing is based on your consent (e.g. your optional country field), withdraw it at any time — this won't affect processing already carried out.
  • Right to lodge a complaint: you can complain to your local data protection supervisory authority at any time. We'd appreciate the chance to address your concern directly first, at the email above.

If you are located outside the EEA/UK — for example under the CCPA in California — you may have similar rights under your own local law; contact us and we'll handle your request under the law that applies to you.

8. Children's Privacy

Under Article 8 GDPR, the age at which a child can consent to an online service without parental approval ranges from 13 to 16 depending on your EU member state. If you are below that age in your country of residence, you may only use RockStyle Chess with the consent of a parent or legal guardian. RockStyle Chess is not directed at young children (ads are requested as general-audience content, rated PG at most), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from a child below the applicable age of consent without such consent having been given. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information without appropriate consent, contact us at royalriffstudio@gmail.com and we will delete it.

9. Data Security

Passwords are hashed, not stored in plain text. Authentication tokens on your device are stored using your operating system's encrypted credential storage. Traffic between the App and our server is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable, industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your information, as required by Article 32 GDPR.

10. Local Storage (No Cookie Banner Needed)

The App does not use browser cookies, and the on-device storage it does use (your authentication token, and local preferences like sound settings) is strictly necessary to sign you in and let you play — it is not used for tracking, profiling, or advertising by us. Under the EU ePrivacy rules, storage that is strictly necessary for a service you've requested is exempt from cookie-style consent requirements. The separate storage and device identifiers used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Section 2i) are not strictly necessary, which is why players in the EEA/UK are shown Google's consent form before any ads are requested.

11. International Data Transfers

Our infrastructure providers host your data in the United States: Railway runs our backend server in its US East region (Virginia), and Neon hosts our database in AWS US East (Ohio). Resend (emails) and the Expo push service (notifications) also process data in the United States. This means personal data of players in the EEA/UK is transferred outside the EEA/UK. Where we transfer personal data of EEA/UK users outside the EEA/UK, we rely on appropriate safeguards recognized under GDPR Chapter V — such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) — to the extent our providers offer them, or another valid transfer mechanism. You can lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority; in Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO). Anonymous crash reports (Section 2j) are stored by Sentry in its EU region (Germany).
